# Seed & Sprout

A cozy, easier take on Threes in a watercolor garden.

- Combine **Seed + Water** to make a **Sprout**, then match two of the same plant to grow it: Forget-me-not, Violet, Daisy, Tulip, Poppy, Rose, Hydrangea, Sunflower, and rare Bouquets and Flower Fields.
- Drag slowly to peek at a move before you let go.
- Easier mode: 3 undos, balanced starters, and one "Pull Weeds" rescue per game.
- Soft breeze sounds, an angelic harp the first time you grow each plant in a game, and quiet background music that grows with your garden. Volumes are in the sound panel.

Play it at https://vector-swan.github.io/seed-and-sprout/ (after GitHub Pages is turned on).

The whole game is one file: `index.html`.
